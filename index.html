A few lines about what I do—and what keeps me interested.

My work sits where software meets infrastructure: designing APIs, building service integrations, and making multi-step workflows more predictable.

I've grown from software engineering and automation at Cisco to cloud infrastructure development at Oracle. Along the way, I completed a Master of Engineering in Engineering Management at Cornell, with technical coursework spanning software engineering, databases, big data, and machine learning.
